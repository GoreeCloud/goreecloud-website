What is GoreeCloud?

More than a homelab.

GoreeCloud is a privacy-first, self-hosted personal and family cloud platform built to preserve a family's digital life over the long term.

The purpose is not to preserve any single server, application, vendor, or architecture forever. The purpose is to preserve the information entrusted to those systems — including memories, records, knowledge, credentials, and personal data — so it remains accessible, portable, recoverable, and understandable as technology changes.

PrivacyProtection is a design requirement, not an optional setting.
OwnershipThe rightful owner retains control over their information.
Open SourceOpen-source, self-hosted software is preferred wherever practical.
RecoverabilityBackups and restoration are part of the architecture.
IndependenceCritical information should not depend on a single provider.
PreservationImportant information should survive technology changes.

The GoreeCloud story

Built deliberately from the beginning.

GoreeCloud started in 2026 as a plan for a private, self-hosted environment and quickly grew into a structured personal cloud platform.

June 1, 2026

Initial planning begins

The first GoreeCloud planning work begins with a goal of replacing subscription-based services with greater privacy, control, and data ownership.

June 7, 2026

The GoreeCloud name is established

GoreeCloud becomes the name for the complete ecosystem: hardware, software, infrastructure, services, configurations, and data.

June 25, 2026

GoreeCloud.com is purchased

The domain becomes the permanent public identity for the project and supports the naming and service-publication needs of the environment.

2026 →

From homelab to documented personal cloud

The project expands into a governed platform with defined architecture, security policies, standards, procedures, recovery requirements, and long-term goals.
