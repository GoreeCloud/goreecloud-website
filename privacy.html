Public website privacy

Privacy starts with collecting less.

The GoreeCloud public website is intentionally static and does not require an account. Its source does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party browser scripts, or external web fonts.

01

No sign-in here

This public information surface does not implement GoreeCloud Identity sessions or authenticated application access.

02

No browser tracking stack

The reviewed website source does not add analytics SDKs, advertising tags, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime JavaScript.

03

Same-origin runtime assets

CSS, JavaScript, and branding used by the deployed site are published with the site rather than fetched by the browser from unrelated origins.

04

External links are explicit

When you choose to leave GoreeCloud for another website, that destination has its own privacy and operational policies.

Authority boundary

Privacy-minded source is not Privacy Shield acceptance.

Privacy Shield is GoreeCloud’s platform privacy authority. This website follows privacy-preserving design choices, but those choices do not manufacture a Privacy Shield runtime acceptance state.

Cloudflare Pages may process ordinary HTTP delivery information as the hosting layer. GoreeCloud should not claim that public hosting produces zero infrastructure metadata.
