GoreeCloud Archive

Preserving the evolution of GoreeCloud.

A curated public record of major milestones, architectural eras, product transitions, and the long-term development of the GoreeCloud personal and family cloud platform.

Foundation era

Ownership, privacy, and recoverability become the core

GoreeCloud begins as a personal-cloud effort centered on direct control of infrastructure, data, software, identity, and long-term preservation.

Platform architecture

Services become a coherent ecosystem

Networking, storage, backups, monitoring, identity, productivity, and public-facing services are organized into defined system roles and architecture boundaries.

Application transition era

Upstream applications become stepping stones

Early GoreeCloud development uses maintained forks and upstream applications to accelerate capability, preserve continuity, and learn product requirements. Over time, first-party domain models, interfaces, data contracts, release engineering, and application logic increasingly move into GoreeCloud-owned source while attribution and migration paths remain preserved.

Suite expansion · 2026

Focused first-party applications multiply

Projects for Mail, Music, Photos, Video, Drive, Sync, Location, Launcher, Calendar, Tasks, Contacts, Notes, Monitor, Network, DNS, Browser, Backup, Identity, Changelogs, and other roles establish a broader GoreeCloud Suite with deliberately separated responsibilities.

Files and device era · 2026

Storage, transfer, location, and device software gain first-party foundations

Drive advances beyond planning into an authorization-first native file platform, Sync establishes a native secure-transfer foundation, Location develops a private multi-user tracking model, Launcher grows into a first-party Android HOME experience, and upstream-derived media and device projects become explicit native-rebuild targets.

Design-system era

Glaze UI becomes the shared design language

GoreeCloud standardizes visual hierarchy, interaction states, accessibility, adaptive layouts, and recognizable product-family presentation across web, mobile, tablet, desktop, and TV experiences. Glaze UI 1.4 becomes the current Stable production target; older consumer pins require controlled migration rather than grandfathered production exceptions.

Security, privacy, and resilience identities

Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep gain distinct platform roles

Security presentation, privacy controls, and long-term resilience receive separate identities so each can evolve without blurring technical authority or product responsibilities. Together with Glaze UI, they are integral platform systems rather than decorative branding.

Platform governance era · August 23, 2026

Current contracts and evidence become part of public truth

Formal platform policies establish that Glaze UI conformance follows the current Stable contract and that Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, and Everkeep claims must remain tied to implemented behavior, current authoritative evidence, and the correct acceptance scope. Unknown, degraded, migration-required, and pending states stay visible instead of being replaced by stronger labels.

Development evidence era · August 24, 2026

Fold identity and the first persistent Drive milestone land

The approved Fold mark becomes Glaze UI's canonical visual identity, GoreeCloud Drive reaches PostgreSQL-backed persistent file/folder metadata CRUD with authorization enforcement, Search advances through RC #09 validation, Notify merges its Linux/Debian and Android client source foundation, and Terminal reaches the 50.2-rc.2 source line. These milestones preserve the distinction between merged source, release candidate, production, and Stable acceptance evidence.

Native application mandate · August 24, 2026

Original GoreeCloud software becomes the required application end state

GoreeCloud formalizes native-from-the-ground-up development as the mandatory product model for every GoreeCloud application. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases become controlled transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Narrow mature technical foundations may remain where replacing them would materially increase security, standards, protocol, codec, rendering, runtime, or interoperability risk. Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep become mandatory Stable-release gates.

Bookmarks native rebuild · August 24, 2026

Bookmarks establishes an original GoreeCloud foundation

An original Go foundation for GoreeCloud Bookmarks is integrated with owner-scoped records, bounded input rules, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. The Linkwarden-derived application and Browser capture path remain transitional continuity surfaces, and no native production cutover is implied by the source milestone.

Capability-family era · 2026

First-party feature systems gain memorable identities

Quill, Waypoint, Beacon, Conduit, Courier, Resonance, and Keepsake organize major application-specific capabilities for Keyboard, Tasks, DNS, Network, Mail, Music, and Photos while keeping the product names intact.

Public web expansion · 2026

GoreeCloud opens a broader public knowledge surface

The main website plus Projects, Design, Privacy, Security, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive create a coordinated Cloudflare Pages portfolio without exposing private operational records.

Archive policy

Public history is curated, not mirrored.

The Archive preserves selected public milestones and context, including superseded development models when they are necessary to understand the project's evolution. It does not mirror internal change logs, credentials, private topology, operational details, personal information, or historical material that was never approved for public release.
