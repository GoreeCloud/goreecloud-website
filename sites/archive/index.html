GoreeCloud Archive

Preserving the evolution of GoreeCloud.

A curated public record of major milestones, architectural eras, product transitions, and the long-term development of the GoreeCloud personal-cloud platform.

Foundation era

Ownership, privacy, and recoverability become the core

GoreeCloud begins as a personal-cloud effort centered on direct control of infrastructure, data, software, identity, and long-term preservation.

Platform architecture

Services become a coherent ecosystem

Networking, storage, backups, monitoring, identity, productivity, and public-facing services are organized into defined system roles and architecture boundaries.

Application era

From third-party tools toward GoreeCloud software

Maintained forks and original native applications begin replacing generic upstream experiences while preserving open-source foundations and migration paths.

Design-system era

Glaze UI becomes the shared design language

GoreeCloud standardizes visual hierarchy, interaction states, accessibility, adaptive layouts, and recognizable product-family presentation across software and websites.

Security and privacy identities

Wardveil Security and Privacy Shield gain defined roles

Platform-wide security presentation and browser-level privacy protection receive distinct identities, contracts, and responsibilities.

Public web expansion

GoreeCloud opens a broader public knowledge surface

Projects, Design, Privacy, Security, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive sites extend the public GoreeCloud presence without exposing private operational records.

Archive policy

Public history is curated, not mirrored.

The Archive preserves selected public milestones and context. It does not mirror internal change logs, credentials, private topology, operational details, personal information, or historical material that was never approved for public release.
