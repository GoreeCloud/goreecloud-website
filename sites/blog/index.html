Development notes, homelab lessons, architecture decisions, product-design work, and public project updates from a privacy-first personal and family cloud ecosystem.

AI

GoreeCloud AI becomes a first-party platform direction

GoreeCloud AI is now defined as the native GoreeCloud conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, research, files, tools, and AI-orchestration application. Ollama remains the local model runtime, while GoreeCloud Search becomes the required first-party current-information and Internet-research provider. Open WebUI and AnythingLLM are replacement targets, and SearXNG remains transitional through the Search replacement path rather than part of the intended long-term AI product boundary.

Source control

Self-hosted Gitea is the planned permanent source-control authority

GoreeCloud is moving toward a self-hosted Gitea development platform for repositories, issues, pull requests, releases, packages, CI/CD state, and GoreeCloud-controlled runners. GitHub remains useful as a migration source and optional external interoperability surface, but the permanent GoreeCloud development model is intended to avoid required GitHub-hosted runner capacity, recurring runner fees, and long-term source-control dependence on an external provider.

Development platform update · August 25, 2026

Platform systems

Mesh and evidence validity tighten platform truth

GoreeCloud Mesh is advancing as the coordination and governance plane for explicit service relationships, dependency context, capability registration, lifecycle events, policy decisions, and evidence-aware interoperability. Glaze UI, Privacy Shield, and Wardveil Security now also encode producer-authoritative evidence-validity boundaries so stale evidence cannot silently support a current conformant, private, or protected state.

Platform architecture update · August 25, 2026

Platform direction

Native applications become the required GoreeCloud end state

GoreeCloud requires every GoreeCloud application to be original native-from-the-ground-up software at the product and application layer. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases are transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Mature libraries, standards engines, codecs, rendering engines, cryptographic primitives, operating-system/runtime components, and comparable foundations may remain when replacing them would materially increase technical risk. Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep are mandatory Stable-release gates across the application portfolio.

Governance and development update · August 24, 2026

Bookmarks

Bookmarks moves into original GoreeCloud code

GoreeCloud Bookmarks has integrated an original Go foundation with owner-scoped bookmark records, bounded URL and metadata validation, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. The Linkwarden-derived application and existing Browser capture path remain transitional continuity surfaces while equivalent native capabilities are completed; no native production cutover is claimed.

Native rebuild update · August 24, 2026

Development pulse

Fold, Drive Milestone 1, and three release-candidate lines move forward

The approved Fold identity is the canonical Glaze UI mark. GoreeCloud Drive has reached persistent file/folder metadata CRUD with PostgreSQL-backed storage and authorization enforcement. Search is validating its current release-candidate line, Notify has merged its first-party Linux/Debian and Android client source foundation, and Terminal is on the 50.2-rc.2 source line. Those current lines retain their separate release, production, Stable, and native-rebuild gates.

Development update · August 24, 2026

Design

Glaze UI Fold becomes the official visual identity

The approved Fold mark is the canonical Glaze UI visual signature. Its authoritative standalone, monochrome, and horizontal-lockup SVGs are maintained with deterministic derivative-generation rules so website, document, profile, favicon, and application artwork all trace back to one source.

Identity update · August 24, 2026

Design

Semantic color becomes a system contract

Glaze UI 1.4 now defines system-wide semantic color roles for accent, surface, selection, focus, success, information, warning, danger, protected, restricted, online, offline, syncing, and unavailable states. Color remains a presentation layer rather than an authority: important meaning must also survive through labels, icons, shapes, patterns, or equivalent non-color cues, and Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep remain authoritative for their own state semantics.

Design-system update · August 24, 2026

Platform

GoreeCloud is becoming a software ecosystem

That ecosystem is governed by a mandatory native application end state. The project spans a growing Suite of original applications and controlled native-rebuild targets connected by Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Mesh, identity, and shared interoperability rules. Open-source infrastructure and mature technical dependencies remain valuable foundations, but complete upstream products are no longer approved permanent GoreeCloud application architectures.

Platform update · August 25, 2026

Governance

Platform names carry explicit implementation contracts

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and Mesh are governed as integral platform systems with distinct authority. Glaze UI uses the current Stable contract for production consumers, while privacy, security, resilience, recovery, preservation, continuity, dependency, and coordination claims are limited to the current implementation and evidence that actually support them. Missing, stale, or outdated required evidence cannot be upgraded by branding or coordination.

Governance update · August 25, 2026

Suite

Drive reaches its first persistent storage milestone

GoreeCloud Drive persists file and folder metadata in PostgreSQL and connects node CRUD to trusted-principal and Space-membership authorization. The milestone keeps fail-closed service enforcement, Drop-only privacy preservation, strict request validation, and the larger authorization-first direction intact.

Drive update · August 24, 2026

Suite

Drive, Sync, Location, Launcher, and Video expand the portfolio

Drive has an authorization-first native storage foundation, Sync has its native service foundation merged, Location is building private multi-user location history and tracking, and Launcher is becoming a first-party Android HOME experience. Video is explicitly a native GoreeCloud rebuild target; Jellyfin-derived code remains transitional compatibility and reference material rather than the approved final product layer.

Release engineering

Search, Notify, and Terminal keep candidate status explicit

Search's current release-candidate line, Notify's merged native-client foundation, and Terminal 50.2-rc.2 are being advanced without collapsing candidate, production, Stable, and native-rebuild states into one label. Search's current SearXNG-derived service and Terminal's current upstream-derived application line remain transitional while original GoreeCloud replacements progress.

Release update · August 24, 2026

Products

First-party capability families stay focused

Mail, Music, Photos, Tasks, DNS, Network, and Keyboard use capability identities such as Courier, Resonance, Keepsake, Waypoint, Beacon, Conduit, and Quill. These names organize first-party feature systems without replacing the applications themselves or substituting branding for implementation.

Platform systems

Brand names do not replace implementation

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and Mesh are integral GoreeCloud platform systems rather than decorative badges. Public claims remain scoped to actual implementation, current authoritative evidence, and the applicable acceptance state; unknown or degraded conditions are not rewritten as reassuring labels.

Suite

Focused apps instead of one giant cloud

GoreeCloud deliberately separates files, synchronization, documents, photos, video, music, mail, AI, location, tasks, calendars, contacts, notes, monitoring, networking, identity, and other responsibilities into focused products that can interoperate without becoming inseparable.

Design

Glaze UI 1.4 is the current Stable production target

Glaze UI 1.4 is the current Stable GoreeCloud design language, carrying deliberate form-factor adaptation across mobile, tablet, desktop, and TV while preserving shared semantics, accessibility expectations, and product consistency. Older consumer pins are migration-required rather than grandfathered production exceptions.

Development

Open-source foundations, native GoreeCloud products

GoreeCloud continues to use mature open-source libraries, standards, protocols, codecs, engines, runtimes, databases, and infrastructure where they are the safer technical choice. At the product layer, however, GoreeCloud applications are required to become original GoreeCloud implementations rather than permanent third-party application forks.

Resilience

Everkeep connects backup to long-term preservation

Backup is only one part of continuity. Everkeep represents the broader expectation that important information remains recoverable, portable, understandable, and transferable even as applications and infrastructure change. Its public status is tied to recovery, validation, portability, preservation, and continuity evidence rather than the Everkeep name alone.

Browser

A dedicated home for GoreeCloud Firefox extensions

GoreeCloud Browser's current Firefox-derived application line is transitional under the native mandate, while mature browser-engine foundations may remain where technically justified. GoreeCloud-maintained Firefox extensions have a dedicated source-control home so browser integrations can be versioned and maintained independently.

Preserving the story behind the platform

Changelogs, the public Archive, the Blog, and the Autobiography project are complementary ways to preserve technical history, decisions, lessons, and the long-term evolution of GoreeCloud.
