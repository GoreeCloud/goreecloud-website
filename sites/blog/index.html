Platform update

GoreeCloud’s public platform map catches up with the software

The current GoreeCloud source inventory now spans 53 repositories: 39 public and 14 private. The public websites have been refreshed around that broader reality, including GoreeCloud AI, Code, Documents, Messenger, Gateway, Quill, and Mesh. Glaze UI 1.5.0 is the current Stable design-system baseline, and the five substantive platform systems are presented through their operational public surfaces: Design Center for Glaze UI, Privacy Center for Privacy Shield, Security Center for Wardveil Security, Continuity Center for Everkeep, and Mesh Center for GoreeCloud Mesh.

Portfolio and website update · August 27, 2026

Developer platform

GoreeCloud Code replaces the idea of a forge as the product boundary

An earlier August 25 direction described self-hosted Gitea as the planned permanent source-control authority. That direction has been superseded. GoreeCloud Code is now the first-party developer and source-control platform for repositories, collaboration, CI/CD, packages, security, and AI-assisted development. Forgejo is the initial replaceable infrastructure foundation: important infrastructure, but not the permanent GoreeCloud product boundary. GitHub can remain useful for migration, public collaboration, mirrors, or interoperability without defining the long-term application architecture.

Source-control direction update · August 27, 2026

Suite

Documents, Messenger, Gateway, and Quill broaden the native portfolio

GoreeCloud Documents is building a first-party intelligent document-management foundation for capture, OCR, search, organization, automation, collaboration, and preservation. Messenger is advancing private messaging, groups, and calling with explicit transport and encryption boundaries. Gateway establishes a native HTTPS ingress, routing, certificate-management, and controlled service-publication layer. Quill continues as the first-party typing, editing, privacy, and intelligent-input capability family used by GoreeCloud Keyboard and related experiences.

Suite development update · August 27, 2026

Design

Glaze UI 1.5 is the current Stable production target

Glaze UI 1.5.0 is now the current Stable GoreeCloud design-system baseline. Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and TV are first-class Stable 1.5 form-factor environments, with governed materials, semantic color, purposeful motion, accessibility requirements, interaction states, responsive layout and density, and current-version adoption rules. Glaze UI 1.4 remains historical and no longer satisfies the current production-target requirement.

Design-system update · August 27, 2026

AI

GoreeCloud AI becomes a first-party platform direction

GoreeCloud AI is defined as the native GoreeCloud conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, research, files, tools, and AI-orchestration application. Ollama remains the local model runtime, while GoreeCloud Search is the required first-party current-information and Internet-research provider. Transitional third-party AI front ends remain replacement targets rather than intended long-term product boundaries.

Platform architecture update · August 25, 2026

Platform direction

Native applications become the required GoreeCloud end state

GoreeCloud requires every GoreeCloud application to be original native-from-the-ground-up software at the product and application layer. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases are transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Mature libraries, standards engines, codecs, rendering engines, cryptographic primitives, operating-system/runtime components, and comparable foundations may remain when replacing them would materially increase technical risk. Current Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep integration remains part of Stable qualification.

Governance and development update · August 24, 2026

Bookmarks

Bookmarks moves into original GoreeCloud code

GoreeCloud Bookmarks has integrated an original Go foundation with owner-scoped bookmark records, bounded URL and metadata validation, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. The Linkwarden-derived application and existing Browser capture path remain transitional continuity surfaces while equivalent native capabilities are completed; no native production cutover is claimed.

Design

Glaze UI Fold becomes the official visual identity

The approved Fold mark is the canonical Glaze UI visual signature. Its authoritative standalone, monochrome, and horizontal-lockup SVGs are maintained with deterministic derivative-generation rules so website, document, profile, favicon, and application artwork all trace back to one source.

Identity update · August 24, 2026

Design

Semantic color becomes a system contract

Glaze UI defines system-wide semantic color roles for accent, surface, selection, focus, success, information, warning, danger, protected, restricted, online, offline, syncing, and unavailable states. Color remains a presentation layer rather than an authority: important meaning must also survive through labels, icons, shapes, patterns, or equivalent non-color cues, and Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Mesh, and application logic remain authoritative for their own underlying state.

Design-system direction · August 24, 2026; current contract carried forward in Glaze UI 1.5

Platform

GoreeCloud is becoming a software ecosystem

The project spans a growing Suite of original applications and controlled native-rebuild targets connected by Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Mesh, identity, and shared interoperability rules. Open-source infrastructure and mature technical dependencies remain valuable foundations, but complete upstream products are not approved permanent GoreeCloud application architectures.

Platform names carry explicit implementation contracts

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and Mesh are integral platform systems with distinct authority. Their public Centers make those responsibilities easier to navigate, but the names and websites do not manufacture design conformance, privacy, security, recoverability, or coordination state. Governance update · August 25–27, 2026

Suite

Drive reaches its first persistent storage milestone

GoreeCloud Drive persists file and folder metadata in PostgreSQL and connects node CRUD to trusted-principal and Space-membership authorization. The milestone keeps fail-closed service enforcement, Drop-only privacy preservation, strict request validation, and the larger authorization-first direction intact.

Drive update · August 24, 2026

Suite

Focused apps instead of one giant cloud

GoreeCloud deliberately separates files, synchronization, documents, photos, video, music, mail, messaging, AI, location, tasks, calendars, contacts, notes, monitoring, networking, identity, developer tooling, and other responsibilities into focused products that can interoperate without becoming inseparable.

Development

Open-source foundations, native GoreeCloud products

GoreeCloud continues to use mature open-source libraries, standards, protocols, codecs, engines, runtimes, databases, and infrastructure where they are the safer technical choice. At the product layer, however, GoreeCloud applications are required to become original GoreeCloud implementations rather than permanent third-party application forks.

Resilience

Everkeep connects backup to long-term preservation

Backup is only one part of continuity. Everkeep represents the broader expectation that important information remains recoverable, portable, understandable, and transferable even as applications and infrastructure change. Its Continuity Center surfaces that model without upgrading unsupported recovery claims.

History

Preserving the story behind the platform

Changelogs, the public Archive, the Blog, and the Autobiography project are complementary ways to preserve technical history, decisions, lessons, superseded directions, and the long-term evolution of GoreeCloud.
