Public Development Roadmap · August 27, 2026

From a homelab into an owned software ecosystem.

GoreeCloud is moving from a collection of self-hosted tools toward a cohesive personal and family cloud whose GoreeCloud application layer is original, native-from-the-ground-up software. Existing upstream applications may remain temporarily for migration, continuity, compatibility, testing, or reference, while narrowly scoped mature technical foundations can remain where replacing them would materially increase security, standards, protocol, codec, rendering, runtime, or interoperability risk. This public roadmap stays intentionally higher level than internal task tracking: dates are not promises, priorities may change, and private infrastructure or security-sensitive work is omitted.

Now

Active development

Work already underway across the platform.

Production

Public web ecosystem

Ten focused public destinations now cover the main GoreeCloud site, Suite, Projects, Design Center, Privacy Center, Security Center, Continuity Center, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive. The current phase is richer information, accessibility, navigation, mobile and desktop refinement, stronger cross-site consistency, and synchronization with the current 53-repository source inventory.

Mandatory direction

Native application rebuild program

Every GoreeCloud application has the same required end state: original GoreeCloud-owned software at the product and application layer. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases are transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Existing production continuity is preserved until native replacements satisfy their separate migration and acceptance gates.

Active development

GoreeCloud AI

GoreeCloud AI is the native AI conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, research, file, tool, and orchestration application. Ollama remains the local model runtime. GoreeCloud Search is the required first-party current-information and Internet-research provider. Retired or transitional third-party AI front ends are replacement targets rather than intended long-term product dependencies.

Milestone 1

GoreeCloud Code

GoreeCloud Code is now the first-party developer and source-control platform for repositories, collaboration, CI/CD, packages, security, and AI-assisted development. Forgejo is the initial replaceable infrastructure foundation, not the permanent GoreeCloud product boundary. Milestone 1 focuses on Forgejo connectivity and a controlled path away from provider-specific assumptions.

Active development

GoreeCloud Mesh

Mesh is the platform coordination and governance plane for explicit service relationships, capability registration, dependency context, policy decisions, lifecycle events, and evidence-aware interoperability. The Mesh Center coordinates these relationships without replacing Manager, Monitor, Network, Gateway, Identity, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, or other specialized authorities.

Active

Native Suite expansion

Manager, Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Documents, Notify, Feed, Mail, Messenger, Music, Changelogs, Drive, Sync, Location, Launcher, Gateway, AI, Code, and other focused applications are advancing as original GoreeCloud software rather than one oversized monolith.

Milestones active

Documents, Messenger, Gateway, and Quill

GoreeCloud Documents is building a Milestone 1 native document-management foundation; GoreeCloud Messenger is advancing private messaging, groups, and calling with explicit transport and encryption boundaries; GoreeCloud Gateway is establishing native HTTPS ingress, routing, certificate, and service-publication control; and GoreeCloud Quill is evolving as the first-party typing, editing, privacy, and intelligent-input capability family.

Active

Transitional application replacement

Browser, Search, Network, DNS, Backup, Bookmarks, Photos, Video, Vault Server, Terminal, Gallery, Memos, and other upstream-derived lines are controlled native-rebuild targets. Their current upstream-derived code may remain only where needed for service continuity, migration, compatibility, rollback, security maintenance, testing, or reference while first-party implementations replace the inherited product layer.

Active

Shared platform architecture

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and GoreeCloud Mesh are substantive platform systems. Their public operational surfaces are the Design Center, Security Center, Privacy Center, Continuity Center, and Mesh Center. Glaze UI 1.5.0 is the current Stable production target; security, privacy, resilience, recovery, preservation, portability, continuity, dependency, and coordination states remain evidence-backed rather than decorative claims.

Stable gate

Platform conformance uplift

No GoreeCloud application qualifies for Stable while a required Glaze UI 1.5, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, or Everkeep integration is missing, incomplete, superseded, outdated, unverified, or unaccepted for its supported scope. Evidence validity is explicit: stale evidence cannot continue supporting a current protected or conformant claim.

Approved identity

Glaze UI Fold identity and semantic color

The approved Fold mark remains the canonical Glaze UI visual identity. Glaze UI 1.5 defines system-wide semantic color roles, materials, motion, accessibility, and Stable Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and TV form-factor semantics while preserving Wardveil, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and Mesh as the authorities for their own underlying state.

Active

Capability families

Application-specific umbrella identities organize major first-party features, including Quill for Keyboard, Waypoint for Tasks, Beacon for DNS, Conduit for Network, Courier for Mail, Resonance for Music, and Keepsake for Photos. Dedicated capability repositories may preserve shared source or identity without turning every capability into a duplicate Suite application.

Milestone 1

Drive persistent storage foundation

GoreeCloud Drive persists file and folder metadata in PostgreSQL and applies trusted-principal plus Space-membership authorization to node creation, reading, updating, and deletion. The milestone preserves fail-closed service enforcement, Drop-only privacy, and strict request validation.

Native foundation merged

Bookmarks moves into original GoreeCloud code

GoreeCloud Bookmarks has integrated an original Go foundation with owner-scoped records, bounded validation, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. The Linkwarden-derived application and existing Browser capture path remain transitional continuity surfaces; no native production cutover is claimed.

Active

Files, transfer, documents, and personal context

Drive has moved beyond initial planning into an authorization-first native storage foundation, Sync has its native foundation merged, Documents is developing a dedicated intelligent document-management layer, and Location is advancing a private multi-user tracking architecture with native Android collection.

RC hardening

Search, Notify, and Terminal candidates

Search continues release-candidate validation while the required native replacement direction remains explicit. Notify has merged its first-party Linux/Debian and Android client source foundation while persistent Android delivery and production cutover remain separate gates. Terminal remains on a release-candidate source line, with Stable approval explicitly separate.

Active

Browser and extension ownership

GoreeCloud Browser continues as a privacy-first transitional Firefox-derived line while moving toward an original GoreeCloud application layer. A dedicated Firefox Extensions repository provides a canonical home for GoreeCloud-maintained browser extensions and integration components.

Near-term priorities

Publicly relevant work expected to follow current foundations.

Planned transition

GoreeCloud Code source-control authority

Advance GoreeCloud Code from Forgejo connectivity into repository migration, issues, pull requests, releases, packages, CI/CD, controlled runners, security workflows, and provider-neutral integrations. Forgejo is the initial replaceable infrastructure foundation; GitHub remains useful for migration, public collaboration, mirrors, or interoperability where appropriate rather than defining the permanent GoreeCloud product boundary.

Planned

GoreeCloud AI Workspaces and native RAG

Advance the initial AI foundation into persistent Workspaces, document ingestion, embeddings, indexing, retrieval, source attribution, privacy-aware research, and representative recovery validation while preserving Ollama as the model runtime and GoreeCloud Search as the controlled current-information provider.

Planned

Native replacement vertical slices

Advance upstream-derived projects through original domain models, authenticated service boundaries, durable storage, migration contracts, portability, recovery, and representative runtime acceptance without prematurely cutting over current services.

Planned

Stable client releases

Expand tested Linux and Android delivery where dedicated clients improve access, while retaining strong web experiences and clear release-lifecycle evidence.

Planned

Integrated Suite experiences

Strengthen deliberate interoperability such as Tasks and Calendar, Drive and Sync, Documents and Drive, Messenger and Contacts, shared identity, notifications, APIs, portability, current-Stable Glaze UI behavior, and the latest approved Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, and Mesh contracts without creating unnecessary coupling.

Planned

Drive, Sync, and document workflows

Continue building the active Drive, Sync, and Documents foundations into durable multi-user file-management, transfer, document intelligence, preservation, and collaboration experiences without recreating an oversized all-in-one cloud product.

Planned

Native device experiences

Advance Launcher, Keyboard, Quill, Terminal, Browser clients, and other device software through build, packaging, accessibility, real-device or workstation acceptance, and controlled release gates.

Planned

Public documentation

Create sanitized, purpose-built guides and architecture explanations without publishing internal records, credentials, private topology, or operationally sensitive detail.

Planned

Release and distribution experience

Make supported versions, release notes, verification guidance, platform packages, and approved download paths easier to understand across GoreeCloud software.

Later

Long-term direction

Strategic outcomes rather than scheduled commitments.

Future

Local-first primary infrastructure

Move the center of gravity from rented infrastructure toward GoreeCloud-owned local systems while preserving resilient remote access, backups, recovery, and portability.

Future

GoreeCloud Code independence

Move normal development, CI/CD, packages, release evidence, repository automation, security workflows, and AI-assisted development behind GoreeCloud Code and GoreeCloud-controlled runners while keeping the underlying forge implementation replaceable and retaining optional external mirrors or collaboration where useful.

Future

Advanced local AI independence

Expand GoreeCloud AI beyond conversational and RAG foundations into justified tools, agents, multimodal capabilities, automation, browser interaction, and local research while keeping models, data, workflows, and knowledge under GoreeCloud control.

Future

Native product ownership

Complete the transition to original GoreeCloud applications at the product layer while retaining only narrowly justified mature technical dependencies and standards-compatible foundations.

Future

Long-term digital continuity

Keep data, memories, documentation, credentials, software, and operational knowledge portable, recoverable, understandable, and transferable across changing technologies.
