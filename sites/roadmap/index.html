Public Development Roadmap · August 2026

From a homelab into an owned software ecosystem.

GoreeCloud is moving from a collection of self-hosted tools toward a cohesive personal and family cloud whose GoreeCloud application layer is original, native-from-the-ground-up software. Existing upstream applications may remain temporarily for migration, continuity, compatibility, testing, or reference, while narrowly scoped mature technical foundations can remain where replacing them would materially increase security, standards, protocol, codec, rendering, runtime, or interoperability risk. This public roadmap stays intentionally higher level than internal task tracking: dates are not promises, priorities may change, and private infrastructure or security-sensitive work is omitted.

Active development

Work already underway across the platform.

Production

Public web ecosystem

The main GoreeCloud site plus Projects, Design, Privacy, Security, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive form the current public Cloudflare Pages portfolio. The current phase is richer information, accessibility, navigation, mobile and desktop refinement, and stronger cross-site consistency.

Mandatory direction

Native application rebuild program

Every GoreeCloud application has the same required end state: original GoreeCloud-owned software at the product and application layer. Maintained forks and adopted complete application codebases are transitional migration, compatibility, testing, historical, or reference sources only. Existing production continuity is preserved until native replacements satisfy their separate migration and acceptance gates.

Initial native development

GoreeCloud AI

GoreeCloud AI is now defined as the native AI conversation, Workspace, knowledge, RAG, research, file, tool, and orchestration application. Ollama remains the local model runtime. GoreeCloud Search is the required first-party current-information and Internet-research provider. Open WebUI, AnythingLLM, and SearXNG are replacement targets rather than intended long-term product dependencies.

Active development

GoreeCloud Mesh

Mesh is the platform coordination and governance plane for explicit service relationships, capability registration, dependency context, policy decisions, lifecycle events, and evidence-aware interoperability. It coordinates Manager, Monitor, Network, Gateway, Identity, and other specialized systems without replacing their authority.

Active

Native Suite expansion

Manager, Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Notify, Feed, Mail, Music, Changelogs, Drive, Sync, Location, Launcher, Messenger, AI, and other focused applications are advancing as original GoreeCloud software rather than one oversized monolith.

Active

Transitional application replacement

Browser, Search, Network, DNS, Backup, Bookmarks, Photos, Video, Vault Server, Terminal, Gallery, Memos, and other upstream-derived lines are controlled native-rebuild targets. Their current upstream-derived code may remain only where needed for service continuity, migration, compatibility, rollback, security maintenance, testing, or reference while first-party implementations replace the inherited product layer.

Active

Shared platform architecture

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, and Mesh are integral systems across the GoreeCloud platform. Glaze UI 1.4 is the current Stable production target; security, privacy, resilience, recovery, preservation, portability, continuity, dependency, and coordination states remain evidence-backed rather than decorative claims.

Stable gate

Platform conformance uplift

No GoreeCloud application qualifies for Stable while a required Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, or Everkeep integration is missing, incomplete, superseded, outdated, unverified, or unaccepted for its supported scope. Evidence validity is now treated explicitly: stale evidence cannot continue supporting a current protected or conformant claim.

Approved identity

Glaze UI Fold identity and semantic color

The approved Fold mark is the canonical Glaze UI visual identity. Glaze UI 1.4 also defines system-wide semantic color roles for status and interaction meaning while requiring non-color cues and preserving Wardveil, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep as the authorities for their own state semantics.

Active

Capability families

Application-specific umbrella identities organize major first-party features, including Quill for Keyboard, Waypoint for Tasks, Beacon for DNS, Conduit for Network, Courier for Mail, Resonance for Music, and Keepsake for Photos.

Milestone 1

Drive persistent storage foundation

GoreeCloud Drive persists file and folder metadata in PostgreSQL and applies trusted-principal plus Space-membership authorization to node creation, reading, updating, and deletion. The milestone preserves fail-closed service enforcement, Drop-only privacy, and strict request validation.

Native foundation merged

Bookmarks moves into original GoreeCloud code

GoreeCloud Bookmarks has integrated an original Go foundation with owner-scoped records, bounded validation, cross-owner isolation tests, native service boundaries, and PostgreSQL persistence/runtime contracts progressing through validated source work. The Linkwarden-derived application and existing Browser capture path remain transitional continuity surfaces; no native production cutover is claimed.

Active

Files, transfer, and personal context

Drive has moved beyond initial planning into an authorization-first native storage foundation, Sync has its native foundation merged, and Location is advancing a private multi-user tracking architecture with native Android collection.

RC hardening

Search, Notify, and Terminal candidates

Search is validating its current release-candidate line while the required native replacement direction remains explicit. Notify has merged its first-party Linux/Debian and Android client source foundation while persistent Android delivery and production cutover remain separate gates. Terminal is on the 50.2-rc.2 source line, with the current implementation transitional and Stable approval still explicitly separate.

Active

Browser and extension ownership

GoreeCloud Browser continues as a privacy-first transitional Firefox-derived line while moving toward an original GoreeCloud application layer. A dedicated Firefox Extensions repository provides a canonical home for GoreeCloud-maintained browser extensions and integration components.

Near-term priorities

Publicly relevant work expected to follow current foundations.

Planned permanent transition

Self-hosted Gitea source control

Gitea is the selected permanent authoritative source-control and development platform. The transition is planned around repository migration, issues, pull requests, releases, packages, Actions-compatible workflows, and GoreeCloud-controlled runners. GitHub remains a transitional migration source and optional external interoperability surface rather than a required long-term dependency.

Planned

GoreeCloud AI Workspaces and native RAG

Advance the initial AI foundation into persistent Workspaces, document ingestion, embeddings, indexing, retrieval, source attribution, privacy-aware research, and representative recovery validation sufficient to replace AnythingLLM while preserving Ollama as the model runtime.

Planned

Native replacement vertical slices

Advance upstream-derived projects through original domain models, authenticated service boundaries, durable storage, migration contracts, portability, recovery, and representative runtime acceptance without prematurely cutting over current services.

Planned

Stable client releases

Expand tested Linux and Android delivery where dedicated clients improve access, while retaining strong web experiences and clear release-lifecycle evidence.

Planned

Integrated Suite experiences

Strengthen deliberate interoperability such as Tasks and Calendar, Drive and Sync, shared identity, notifications, APIs, portability, current-Stable Glaze UI behavior, and the latest approved Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, and Mesh contracts without creating unnecessary coupling.

Planned

Drive, Sync, and document workflows

Continue building the active Drive and Sync foundations into durable multi-user file-management and transfer experiences, while developing a dedicated GoreeCloud Documents direction without recreating an oversized all-in-one cloud product.

Planned

Native device experiences

Advance Launcher, Keyboard, Terminal, Browser clients, and other device software through build, packaging, accessibility, real-device or workstation acceptance, and controlled release gates.

Planned

Public documentation

Create sanitized, purpose-built guides and architecture explanations without publishing internal records, credentials, private topology, or operationally sensitive detail.

Planned

Release and distribution experience

Make supported versions, release notes, verification guidance, platform packages, and approved download paths easier to understand across GoreeCloud software.

Later

Long-term direction

Strategic outcomes rather than scheduled commitments.

Future

Local-first primary infrastructure

Move the center of gravity from rented infrastructure toward GoreeCloud-owned local systems while preserving resilient remote access, backups, and recovery.

Future

Complete Gitea independence

Complete the migration of normal development, CI/CD, packages, release evidence, repository automation, and AI-assisted development to GoreeCloud-controlled Gitea and self-hosted runners while retaining optional mirrors or public collaboration where useful.

Future

Advanced local AI independence

Expand GoreeCloud AI beyond conversational and RAG foundations into justified tools, agents, multimodal capabilities, automation, browser interaction, and local research while keeping models, data, workflows, and knowledge under GoreeCloud control.

Future

Native product ownership

Complete the transition to original GoreeCloud applications at the product layer while retaining only narrowly justified mature technical dependencies and standards-compatible foundations.

Future

Long-term digital continuity

Keep data, memories, documentation, credentials, software, and operational knowledge portable, recoverable, understandable, and transferable across changing technologies.
