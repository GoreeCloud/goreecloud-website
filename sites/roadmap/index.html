Public Development Roadmap · August 2026

From a homelab into an owned software ecosystem.

GoreeCloud is actively moving from a collection of self-hosted tools toward a cohesive personal and family cloud built from native applications, maintained open-source forks, shared platform systems, and independently recoverable data. This public roadmap stays intentionally higher level than internal task tracking: dates are not promises, priorities may change, and private infrastructure or security-sensitive work is omitted.

Now

Active development

Work already underway across the platform.

Production

Public web ecosystem

The main GoreeCloud site plus Projects, Design, Privacy, Security, Roadmap, Blog, and Archive form the current public Cloudflare Pages portfolio. The current phase is richer information, accessibility, navigation, mobile and desktop refinement, and stronger cross-site consistency.

Active

Native Suite expansion

Manager, Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Notify, Feed, Mail, Music, Changelogs, GitHub Dashboard, Drive, Sync, Location, and Launcher are being developed as focused GoreeCloud applications rather than one oversized monolith.

Active

Fork-to-native evolution

Browser, Search, Network, DNS, Backup, Bookmarks, Photos, Video, Vault Server, Terminal, and other maintained forks are being hardened while GoreeCloud-owned interfaces and capabilities progressively replace upstream product identity where that improves long-term ownership.

Active

Shared platform architecture

Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Identity, and the GoreeCloud Suite authority define reusable design, security, privacy, resilience, authentication, portfolio, and interoperability conventions across the ecosystem. Glaze UI, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep are integral platform systems, not decorative labels.

Active

Capability families

Application-specific umbrella identities organize major first-party features, including Quill for Keyboard, Waypoint for Tasks, Beacon for DNS, Conduit for Network, Courier for Mail, Resonance for Music, and Keepsake for Photos.

Active

Files, transfer, and personal context

Drive has moved beyond initial planning into an authorization-first native storage foundation, Sync has its native foundation merged, and Location is advancing a private multi-user tracking architecture with native Android collection.

Active

Browser and extension ownership

GoreeCloud Browser continues as a privacy-first Firefox-based browser while a dedicated Firefox Extensions repository provides a canonical home for GoreeCloud-maintained browser extensions and integration components.

Next

Near-term priorities

Publicly relevant work expected to follow current foundations.

Planned

Stable client releases

Expand tested Linux and Android delivery where dedicated clients improve access, while retaining strong web experiences and clear release-lifecycle evidence.

Planned

Integrated Suite experiences

Strengthen deliberate interoperability such as Tasks and Calendar, Drive and Sync, shared identity, notifications, APIs, portability, and consistent Glaze UI behavior without creating unnecessary coupling.

Planned

Drive, Sync, and document workflows

Continue building the active Drive and Sync foundations into durable multi-user file-management and transfer experiences, while developing a dedicated GoreeCloud Documents direction without recreating an oversized all-in-one cloud product.

Planned

Native device experiences

Advance Launcher, Keyboard, Terminal, browser clients, and other device software through build, packaging, accessibility, real-device or workstation acceptance, and controlled release gates.

Planned

Public documentation

Create sanitized, purpose-built guides and architecture explanations without publishing internal records, credentials, private topology, or operationally sensitive detail.

Planned

Release and distribution experience

Make supported versions, release notes, verification guidance, platform packages, and approved download paths easier to understand across GoreeCloud software.

Planned

Project history and storytelling

Expand the public Blog, Archive, Changelogs, and Autobiography surfaces so GoreeCloud's technical decisions, lessons, milestones, and long-term evolution remain understandable over time.

Later

Long-term direction

Strategic outcomes rather than scheduled commitments.

Future

Local-first primary infrastructure

Move the center of gravity from rented infrastructure toward GoreeCloud-owned local systems while preserving resilient remote access, backups, and recovery.

Future

Self-hosted source control

Move toward GoreeCloud-controlled source hosting while retaining GitHub for public collaboration, mirrors, and wider open-source participation.

Future

Local AI independence

Expand locally operated AI, research, and automation capabilities while keeping models, data, workflows, and knowledge under GoreeCloud control.

Future

Native product ownership

Continue the transition from third-party-facing product layers toward original GoreeCloud applications or independently maintained forks wherever that materially improves ownership, maintainability, privacy, or continuity.

Future

Long-term digital continuity

Keep data, memories, documentation, credentials, software, and operational knowledge portable, recoverable, understandable, and transferable across changing technologies.

Roadmap principles

Direction without unnecessary promises.
